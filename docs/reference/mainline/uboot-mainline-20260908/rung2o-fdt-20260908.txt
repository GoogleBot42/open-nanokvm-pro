  0x7e68c000 is at ~1006 MB. The cmdline says mem=512M, so the kernel's memory
  ends at 0x60000000. U-Boot relocates the FDT against its own ram_top, which is
  the real 1 GiB, and hands the kernel a device tree it cannot map. It dies
  before any console and before ramoops -- which is why pstore was empty every
  time.

  Fix: fdt_high = 0x5f000000, a real ceiling under the kernel's limit.
