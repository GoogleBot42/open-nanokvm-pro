The complete mainline U-Boot console log from the AX630C, read out of DRAM
after the fact. This board's UART is on hidden pads; there is no serial.

How it was captured (pkgs/uboot-mainline.nix, debugMilestones = true):

  * CONFIG_PRE_CONSOLE_BUFFER at 0x480E8000, 8 KiB, inside the spare tail of
    the vendor pstore window.
  * A third mm_region mapping 0x48000000+1 MiB as MT_DEVICE_NGNRNE, so the
    buffer keeps reaching DRAM after dcache_enable() -- without it every
    character written past initr_caches sits in a cache that a chip reset
    discards.
  * board_late_init() clears GD_FLG_HAVE_CONSOLE, which makes puts() take the
    pre_console_putc() path for the whole rest of the boot. print_pre_console_
    buffer() restores precon_buf_idx after its flush, so the buffer stays live.

Read back from slot A with:
  dd if=/dev/mem bs=4096 skip=$((0x480e8000/4096)) count=2 | tr -d '\000'

The `mmu:` and `ram_base`/`probe` lines are this build's instrumentation;
everything else is stock U-Boot.
