Our own drivers/watchdog/ax630c_wdt.c stops the dog at probe. A kernel that
gets that far and then fails has disabled the only channel that survives a
hang, and the power cycle that recovers the board destroys the console.

Consistent with the kernel booting; not proof, and not claimed. The next
attempt should keep the watchdog driver out of the boot (initcall_blacklist or
a cmdline that omits it) so a failed hand-off still resets and still banks.
